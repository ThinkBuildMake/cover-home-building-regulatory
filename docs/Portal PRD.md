PRD

For residential construction projects, determining what can be built on a specific parcel is complex due to distributed zoning codes and regulatory documents that require interpretation. The challenge is to produce an evidence-backed buildability assessment that synthesizes multiple sources into clear, actionable constraints while handling ambiguous regulations and maintaining continuous refinement as project inputs change.

Problem & Context

Business Context

At Cover, this project aims to transform the manual, slow process of regulatory research into an automated system. This will accelerate design exploration, improve client conversations, and reduce entitlement surprises. The goal is to make high-quality homebuilding more predictable and accessible while enhancing client experience.

Impact Metrics

- Reduce regulatory research effort to zero
- Real-time buildability answers as inputs change
- Consistent cited regulatory answers across parcels

Requirements & Success Criteria

Functional Requirements

- Interface for entering residential address/APN
- Real-time structured buildability assessment
- Parcel and existing building visualization
- Support for multiple building types (Single Family Home, ADU, Guest House)
- Confidence signals and citation tracking
- Clear distinction between deterministic and inferred conclusions

Performance Benchmarks

Real-time response for buildability assessments (inferred from requirements), Scalable architecture to handle LA City, LA County, and broader regions

Code Quality Expectations
